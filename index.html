WHO WE ARE

Building Skills.
Changing Futures.

Bano Qabil is an IT education and youth development initiative associated with Alkhidmat. Its mission is to help young people develop practical digital skills and explore opportunities in the technology industry.

The program offers IT training, learning opportunities and a pathway towards employment, freelancing and continued professional development.

OUR JOURNEY

A Vision for a Digital Future.

Bano Qabil emerged as an Alkhidmat youth development initiative focused on making practical IT education accessible to young people, particularly in Karachi.

Over time, the program's online presence has included technical training, creative skills, digital entrepreneurship and student services. Its official portals publish course catalogs, campus information, announcements and learning updates.

For a complete year-by-year timeline, founding date, verified cumulative graduate figures and official leadership history, consult the organization's published records.
